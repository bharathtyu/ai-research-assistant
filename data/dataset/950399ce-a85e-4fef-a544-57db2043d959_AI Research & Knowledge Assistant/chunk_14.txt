Note 
You are encouraged to use publicly available libraries, frameworks, and AI-assisted 
development tools. 
However, during the technical interview, you will be expected to: 
●​ Explain your implementation 
●​ Justify design decisions 
●​ Modify portions of your code 
The evaluation will prioritize your understanding of the system, code quality, 
architectural decisions, and problem-solving approach over the use of any 
specific library or framework.
