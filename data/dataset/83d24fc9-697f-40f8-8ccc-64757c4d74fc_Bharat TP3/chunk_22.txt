We conduct experiments on two facial recognition datasets: LFW (Labeled Faces in the Wild) 
and CelebA. LFW contains 13,000 images of 5,749 people, and CelebA contains 202,599 
images of 10,177 celebrities. We use VGG-16 and ResNet-50 as pretrained CNN models for 
feature extraction and fine-tuning, respectively. We compare the performance of fine-tuning and 
feature extraction with a linear classifier, and we vary the number of training samples from 50 
to 5,000.
