Face Recognition 
On the internet, there are a plethora of face recognition techniques to choose from. Choosing 
the right technique for the job, on the other hand, is the most difficult part of the project. We 
began with a deep learning-based local neural network for extracting facial features and 
computing embeddings. After that, the embeddings were used as a training input for the SVM 
classifier. The trained model was stored locally and then deployed for recognition later. 
Although the performance was satisfactory when tested on two individuals, the accuracy 
dropped as the input dataset grew larger. In addition, we experimented with the local DNN by 
adding additional convolutional layers, including max pooling and dropout layers, using various 
optimizers, and altering the DNN’s input image size. We even used a face aligner, and
