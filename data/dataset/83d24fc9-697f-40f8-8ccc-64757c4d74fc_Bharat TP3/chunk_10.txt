In the era of rapid technological evolution, the automation of various facets of daily life has 
become ubiquitous, driven by continuous improvements in machine learning. Among the 
prominent applications, face recognition stands out as a transformative technology aimed 
at identifying and verifying individuals based on their unique facial features. This paper 
presents a comprehensive overview of key methodologies employed in face recognition 
using machine learning techniques. In essence, this paper serves as a comprehensive guide 
to the multifaceted realm of face recognition through machine learning, offering valuable 
insights into the intricacies of algorithmic approaches, their applications, and the evolving 
landscape of this transformative technology.
